This marks the first time an AI has autonomously generated a proof of a long-standing unsolved conjecture, potentially transforming how mathematical research is conducted and raising questions about the role of AI in theoretical discovery. The proof is reportedly extremely concise, suggesting a clever trick that experts had missed, and the full prompt used to guide the model was also released, providing insight into the interaction between human guidance and AI reasoning.

GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra is OpenAI's latest model, featuring an 'ultra' mode that coordinates multiple sub-agents for complex tasks.

## [Relativity governs chemical bonds in heavy elements](https://www.brown.edu/news/2026-07-09/chemical-bonds-relativity) ⭐️ 8.0/10

New research experimentally confirms that Einstein's theory of relativity governs chemical bonds in heavy elements through spin-orbit coupling, as published in Science. This provides the first direct experimental evidence for relativistic effects in chemical bonding, bridging physics and chemistry and deepening our understanding of heavy-element behavior. The study focuses on spin-orbit coupling, where an electron's spin and orbital motion become interdependent at relativistic speeds, altering bond types like sigma and pi bonds in heavy elements.

hackernews · hhs · Jul 10, 22:30 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48866134)

**Background**: In heavy atoms, electrons near the nucleus travel at a significant fraction of the speed of light, making relativistic effects important. Spin-orbit coupling, a relativistic interaction, splits energy levels and influences chemical bonding. While known theoretically, experimental confirmation has been challenging.
