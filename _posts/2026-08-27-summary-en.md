## [FDA Approves First-in-Class Targeted Therapy for Metastatic Pancreatic Cancer](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-class-targeted-therapy-metastatic-pancreatic-cancer) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The FDA has approved the first-in-class targeted therapy for metastatic pancreatic cancer, specifically a KRAS inhibitor, marking a major breakthrough in treating a previously 'undruggable' cancer target. The approval came just over a month after the FDA accepted the new drug application, facilitated by the CNPV Pilot Program. This approval is significant because pancreatic cancer has historically poor outcomes, and this new therapy offers a targeted option for patients with KRAS mutations. It also paves the way for similar KRAS inhibitors to be approved for other cancers, potentially transforming treatment across multiple tumor types. The drug targets the KRAS G12C mutation, covalently binding to the cysteine residue at position 12, which is unique to this mutation and spares normal KRAS. The approval was notably fast, with the FDA's decision coming just over a month after acceptance of the NDA, compared to typical 8-12 month timelines.

hackernews · leopoldj · Aug 26, 16:19 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49451675)

**Background**: KRAS is a gene that, when mutated, can drive cancer growth and is found in a substantial fraction of cancers across many organs. For decades, KRAS was considered 'undruggable' because its surface lacks obvious pockets for traditional small-molecule inhibitors. However, recent advances have led to the development of KRAS G12C inhibitors that exploit a specific pocket in the switch II region, enabling targeted therapy.

## [Qwen3.8-Flash-Next: 125B MoE Model Previews Qwen4 Architecture](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/26/qwen38-flash-next/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Qwen released Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, a multimodal Mixture-of-Experts model with 125B total parameters and 6B active parameters per token, serving as an early preview of the Qwen4 architecture. Simon Willison tested it on an NVIDIA DGX Spark using Unsloth quantized GGUF models, generating images of pelicans. This release is significant because it is the first open-weights model built on the Qwen4 architecture, offering a glimpse into the future of Qwen's model design. Its large total parameter count with sparse activation enables high performance while remaining feasible to run locally on high-end hardware, which is valuable for the AI community. The model has 125B total parameters plus an additional 51B N-gram embeddings, with only 6B parameters activated per token. Simon Willison tested the 72.5GB UD-IQ1_S and 78.9GB UD-Q2_K_XL Unsloth quantizations, noting that the latter produced his favorite results with high reasoning effort.

rss · Simon Willison · Aug 26, 23:52

**Background**: Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models use sparse activation, meaning only a subset of parameters are active for each token, allowing them to have a large total parameter count while maintaining computational efficiency. Qwen is a series of open-weights AI models developed by Alibaba, and this release is an early preview of the upcoming Qwen4 architecture. Quantization techniques like Unsloth's reduce model size for local deployment, often with minimal quality loss.
