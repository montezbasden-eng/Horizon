## [Isar Aerospace's Spectrum Rocket Reaches Orbit from Norway](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/isar-aerospace-second-launch-norway-andoya-spaceport-spectrum-rocket) ⭐️ 8.0/10

German startup Isar Aerospace successfully launched its Spectrum rocket from Andøya Spaceport in Norway, achieving orbit and deploying payloads on its second flight. This marks the first orbital launch by a private company from continental Europe. This achievement provides Europe with an independent, sovereign launch capability from its own soil, reducing reliance on foreign launch providers and the distant Guiana Space Centre. It could boost launch cadence and lower costs, strengthening the European space industry's competitiveness. The Spectrum rocket is a two-stage small-satellite launcher designed for payloads up to 1,000 kg to low Earth orbit. The successful flight follows an earlier test in March 2025 that ended in failure shortly after liftoff due to an unintended vent valve opening.

The success of Spectrum demonstrates the viability of small launchers and private enterprise in the European space sector.
